Precision Innovation

Design Highlights of the BMW 3 Series

HIGHLIGHT 01 // INTERIOR

BMW Curved Display

A single, continuous frameless glass display uniting a 12.3" instrument cluster with a 14.9" control display, angled ergonomically toward the driver with touch and voice operation.

HIGHLIGHT 02 // EXTERIOR

Active Air Stream Kidney

Sculpted double slats and active air flaps that open only when additional cooling air is required, dramatically optimizing drag coefficient down to 0.23 Cd.

HIGHLIGHT 03 // DYNAMICS

M Sport Differential

Electronically controlled multi-disc limited-slip differential that dynamically varies lockup from 0 to 100%, stabilizing wheel rotation for maximum traction out of apex corners.
